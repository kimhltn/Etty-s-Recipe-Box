# Quick Salsa Verde Chicken & Rice Soup — Cook Mode

*The caldo's 30-minute cousin: rotisserie chicken, no poach, no charred-tomato base — a ginger and salsa verde fry does the heavy lifting. Rice cooks alongside; the soup takes exactly as long as the rice does. For the full Sunday version, see `recipes/caldo-de-pollo-con-arroz.md`.*

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep:** 5 min | **Cook:** ~25 min | **Total:** ~30 min

---

## 🛒 Grocery List

### Produce
- [ ] Fresh ginger (need 1 tbsp minced)
- [ ] 1 bunch cilantro (need a big handful, chopped)
- [ ] 1 bunch green onions (need 2–3)
- [ ] 1 lemon (or lime)
- [ ] 1 avocado

### Meat & Dairy
- [ ] ½ rotisserie chicken (~2 cups shredded)

### Pantry
- [ ] 1 cup white rice (basmati or long-grain) — *1½ cups if you want a second meal's worth*
- [ ] Neutral oil (2 tbsp)
- [ ] 3–4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- [ ] Fresh salsa verde, store-bought (2 tbsp)
- [ ] 1 jar diced green chilies (need 1 tbsp)
- [ ] Kosher salt

---

## Before You Start (5-Minute Prep)

- **Rice:** rinse 1 cup and get it going first — stovetop, rice cooker, whatever you use. It's the clock for this recipe.
- **Ginger:** mince 1 tbsp.
- **Chicken:** pull the meat off half a rotisserie chicken and shred — about 2 cups. Discard skin and bones (or freeze the bones for stock).
- *Cilantro, green onion, lemon, and avocado all get cut while the broth simmers — don't do them now.*

---

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Rice On (start this first)

1. Cook **1 cup rice** by your usual method, about **20 minutes** plus a rest. Everything below happens while it cooks.

### Step 2 — Fry the Base

1. In a large saucepan, heat **2 tbsp neutral oil** over medium. Add **1 tbsp minced ginger**, **2 tbsp salsa verde**, and **1 tbsp jarred diced green chilies**. Fry, stirring, **3–4 minutes**, until the salsa has darkened slightly and the raw-oniony smell has turned toasty.

### Step 3 — Simmer

1. Pour in **3–4 cups chicken broth** (3 for a brothy-but-substantial bowl, 4 to stretch it) and bring to a simmer. Simmer uncovered **10 minutes** while the rice finishes. *(Now: chop a big handful of cilantro, slice 2–3 green onions, halve the lemon, slice the avocado.)*
2. Taste and salt — store broth and rotisserie chicken vary a lot, so this can need anything from nothing to a full teaspoon.

### Step 4 — Chicken & Finish (last 5 minutes)

1. Add the **shredded chicken** and heat through, **5 minutes** — no longer, or it dries out.
2. Off heat, stir in the **chopped cilantro**, **sliced green onions**, and the **juice of ½ lemon**. Taste once more.

### Step 5 — Serve

1. Spoon a mound of **rice** into each bowl and ladle the broth and chicken over.
2. Top with **sliced avocado**.

---

## Tips

- **Store rice and soup separately** — same rule as the caldo; rice left in the broth drinks it up overnight.
- **Reheating:** warm the soup, add rice for the last minute. First cook used 1½ cups rice and there was broth and chicken to match — it makes a clean second meal.
- **Lime works** in place of lemon and reads more classically Mexican; lemon is what the first cook used and it was great.
- **More heat:** a second tbsp of green chilies, or a few drops of habanero sauce at the table.
- **Upgrade the rice:** if there's time, the Spanish basmati from `recipes/spanish-basmati-rice.md` turns this into something close to the full caldo for 10 extra minutes.
